DMCA – Font Changer

Welcome to Font Changer! We respect intellectual property rights and are committed to responding to valid copyright concerns. If you believe any content on our website infringes your rights, you can contact us as described below.

font-changer.com (“Site”, “we”) complies with the U.S. Digital Millennium Copyright Act (17 U.S.C. § 512). We respond to properly formatted DMCA notices by removing or disabling access to infringing material and may take action against repeat infringers where appropriate.

1) How to Send a DMCA Takedown Notice

If you believe your copyrighted work has been used without permission, please send a written DMCA notice including the following details:

  • Your full name and electronic or physical signature.
  • Clear identification of the copyrighted work being infringed.
  • The exact URL(s) or location of the infringing material on our website.
  • Your contact information (name, address, email, and phone number).
  • A statement confirming your good-faith belief that the use is not authorized.
  • A statement confirming the accuracy of the information and your authority to act on behalf of the copyright owner.

Designated Email: your-email@example.com

Subject Line: DMCA Notice – font-changer.com

2) Response to DMCA Notices

After receiving a valid DMCA request, we may remove or disable access to the reported content and notify the user responsible where applicable.

3) Counter-Notification

If you believe content was removed by mistake or misidentification, you may submit a counter-notice including your contact details, identification of the removed content, and a statement of good-faith belief.

If a valid counter-notice is received, we may restore the content unless the original complainant files legal action within the required timeframe.

4) Repeat Infringers

We reserve the right to restrict or terminate access for users who repeatedly violate copyright policies.

5) Misuse of DMCA

Submitting false or misleading DMCA claims may result in legal consequences under applicable laws, including liability for damages.

6) Contact Us

For DMCA-related concerns, please contact us directly via our official contact page or email provided above. Requests sent to third parties may not be processed.
